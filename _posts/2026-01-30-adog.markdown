## The Setup

I shared with Claude a collection of documents spanning four years of thought and work:

- **Uncannis Familiaris** (2021/2022) — my Berkeley cognitive science thesis on human-canine relations, psychedelics, and the possibility of interspecies communication
- **The First 10 Trillion Dollar "Company" Will Be an Indian Healthcare Alliance** (2025) — an essay on UPI-style protocols for healthcare and the vision of a mycelial network powering India's health transformation
- **Dognosis Seed Deck V2** (2025) — the pitch for our $5M raise, 5x Stage-1 sensitivity of GRAIL at 1/100th the cost
- **BreathEasy JCO Preprint** (2025) — the clinical validation, 1,502 participants, AUC 0.962, seven dogs named Chloe, Cheetah, Iggy, Billy, Whiskey, Jessie, and Bane
- **Cosmic Birthday Gift** (2021) — an essay on mystical experience, the prison-house of language, and becoming authors of the paranormal

What follows is the conversation that emerged.
